What ProjectVybe is

A place for practical technology progress.

ProjectVybe brings together practical software projects, AI/ML and computer science project ideas, training, mentorship, documentation support, and software development services in one place.

Our goal is to make it easier to explore a project, understand what it involves, find the right support, and take the next step with confidence.
